# Journal - 2026-08-29 - Day 6: From Working Code to Engineering

## Today in one sentence

## What I learned
- One idea that really stayed with me was **Working ≠ Finished**. In collaborative work, something is not truly finished just because it runs on my end. Other people should also be able to understand how it works and why certain decisions were made.

- This made me appreciate the importance of **good documentation**, especially documenting the things that the code itself cannot explain, such as design decisions, assumptions, context, and how the system should be used.

- We also started working more with **Git and GitHub** as tools for collaboration. I am still very new to GitHub, but I am starting to understand why version history and meaningful commits matter when several people are working on the same project.
